eBOSS is a research assistant for understanding Davao City government services in English, Taglish, and Bislish.

01

Ask naturally

Name your service and describe what you need. Each question is processed independently, so include the relevant details again in a follow-up.

02

Find the evidence

The assistant searches passages from the study’s charter documents and uses those passages to prepare an answer.

03

Check your next step

Open the cited pages and confirm details with the office. An answer can be incomplete or incorrect even when it includes a citation.

Before you ask

This prototype does not submit applications, issue permits, collect payments, or provide official decisions. If your question is ambiguous, it may ask you to clarify.

Keep personal identifiers and private documents out of your question. The chat stays in this open page; this website does not save your questions to a server history. Hosting infrastructure may keep access logs.

Built as part of the LGSDeliveryRAGAssistant thesis project. Study results and implementation are available in the research repository ↗.
