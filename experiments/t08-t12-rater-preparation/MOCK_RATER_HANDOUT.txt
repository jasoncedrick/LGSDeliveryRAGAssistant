BLIND EXPERT GRADING: PRACTICE HANDOUT
Dictionary-assisted hybrid RAG thesis | Orientation exercise only

ALL SERVICES, SOURCE EXCERPTS, ANSWERS AND CITATIONS BELOW ARE FICTIONAL.
They are training materials, not Davao City procedures or actual model outputs.
Do not include these practice scores in the final study dataset.

YOUR TASK

Read the citizen's question, the reference evidence, and each anonymous answer.
Give EACH answer TWO separate scores:
  - Answer Relevance: Does it address what the citizen asked?
  - Factual Faithfulness: Are its factual claims supported by the source?

Use the rubric for each answer independently. Both answers may receive the
same score. You do not have to pick a winner. A fluent or lengthy answer does
not automatically deserve a high score.

During the real session, work independently. Do not discuss scores or try to
identify the system. Cite the statement or source passage behind your judgment.
If evidence is missing, contradictory, or unreadable, flag the item for review
instead of guessing. The facilitator will explain the study's missing-score
procedure before formal scoring begins.

FOUR-POINT RUBRIC
Condensed from manuscript Table 2, printed page 30 (PDF page 35).

Score | Answer Relevance
  1   | Does not address the inquiry, or answers a different question.
  2   | Addresses part of the inquiry but misses the primary procedural need.
  3   | Addresses the main inquiry with limited minor omissions.
  4   | Directly and comprehensively addresses all parts of the inquiry.

Score | Factual Faithfulness
  1   | Main answer is unsupported or contradicts the official source.
  2   | Some claims are supported, but there is a major error in a fee,
      | requirement, or office.
  3   | Main claims are supported, with minor imprecisions that do not
      | change the procedure.
  4   | All factual claims are supported and traceable to official sources.

An answer can score differently on the two dimensions. Missing information
primarily affects completeness/relevance. Incorrect assertions affect factual
faithfulness. Apply the approved rubric; do not average the two dimensions.
