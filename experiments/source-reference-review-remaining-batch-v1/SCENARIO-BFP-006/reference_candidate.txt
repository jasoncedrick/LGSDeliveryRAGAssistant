The BFP public warning says it does not accredit or recommend any brand, store or distributor of fire extinguishers/fire-fighting equipment. Its warning prohibits personnel from selling or recommending a specific fire-extinguisher brand.

The image panel says portable fire extinguishers are regulated by DTI through BPS and that each type must comply with appropriate Philippine National Standards for product safety/reliability. This supports no BFP brand accreditation; it should not be expanded into a claim that any product is suitable regardless of type or hazard.
