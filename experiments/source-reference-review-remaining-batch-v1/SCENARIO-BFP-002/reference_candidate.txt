For FSIC for Certificate of Occupancy and new/renewal business applications, the fee panel states FSIF is 15% of all fees charged by LGU/PEZA, not less than PHP500. It explicitly adds: if assessment from LGUs/PEZA is zero, FSIF is zero. Other fees apply if applicable under the cited RIRR provision. Do not omit the zero-assessment exception or narrow the base to LGU-only regulatory fees.
