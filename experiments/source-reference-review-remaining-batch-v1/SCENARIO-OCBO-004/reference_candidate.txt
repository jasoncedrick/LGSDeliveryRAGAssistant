While the Building Permit is being processed, the Building Official may issue GP&EP for foundation subject to line-and-grade verification, inspection and review. It is valid only for 30 days or superseded upon Building Permit issuance. Fees: inspection/verification PHP200; excavation PHP3 per cubic meter; GP&EP issuance PHP50; foundation excavation with basement PHP4 per cubic meter; excavation other than foundation/basement PHP3 per cubic meter; permitted footing/foundation encroachment into public areas PHP250 per square meter or fraction thereof.
