Fencing checklist: permit form with 3 sets of Civil/Architectural plans signed/sealed by licensed Civil Engineer/Architect (A3 structural-plan option stated); certified land title and property tax declaration (1 original and 1 photocopy each); the next row repeats Certified True Copy of Tax Declaration of the Property with CTO as source; Barangay Clearance (1 original and 1 photocopy); scaled sketch/lot/vicinity plan signed/sealed by licensed Geodetic Engineer (3 sets); bill of materials/cost estimates signed/sealed by licensed Civil Engineer (3 sets); signing professionals PRC ID/current PTR (1 photocopy); owner/applicant ID with 3 specimen signatures (1 photocopy); conditional DPWH clearance along a National Highway.

For fencing made of masonry, metal or concrete: height <= 1.80 m costs PHP3.00 per linear meter; height > 1.80 m costs PHP4.00 per linear meter. Both printed source rows include "of fraction thereof". For indigenous materials, barbed, chicken or hog wire, the listed rate is PHP2.40 per linear meter. No additional height qualifier is stated in that row. The service introduction says not exceeding 1.80 m, while the fee table explicitly includes a higher-height rate; this candidate reproduces the fee table without resolving that scope discrepancy.
