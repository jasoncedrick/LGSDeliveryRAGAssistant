The Professional Tax service lists a TOTAL fee of PHP300. Requirements: unexpired PRC/IBP ID (1 original); PRC/IBP certification for new registrants/passers (1 original); current Davao City address and valid contact number/email address.

Register/log in at ptr.davaocity.gov.ph, generate an Order of Payment, then pay at a Miscellaneous Fees counter or the listed Landbank payment portal.
