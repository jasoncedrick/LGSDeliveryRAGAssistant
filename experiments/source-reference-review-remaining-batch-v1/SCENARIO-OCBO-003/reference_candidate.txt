Occupancy checklist: application letter (1 original); authorization letter if applicant is not owner (1 original); owner ID with 3 specimen signatures (1 photocopy); Certificate of Occupancy (1 original); Certificate of Completion (1 original); BFP Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (1 original); four-elevation project pictures (front/back/left/right); approved Building Construction, Architectural, Civil/Structural, Sanitary/Plumbing, Electrical and Mechanical installation permits (1 photocopy); approved building-permit plan (1 original).

If a different professional undertook/was assigned the project, the checklist requires a signed, notarized waiver from the previous professional in charge (1 original). It also lists Structure Images with CAAP clearance and structural survey for actual building height, signed/sealed by a licensed Geodetic Engineer. The authorization-letter source cell says CPDO and the picture source cell says Hired Geodetic Engineer; those unusual source associations are preserved.
