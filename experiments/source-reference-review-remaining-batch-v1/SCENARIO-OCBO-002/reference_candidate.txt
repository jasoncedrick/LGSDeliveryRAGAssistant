Conditional requirements state: construction/minor repair of buildings 3 storeys and above requires Construction Safety & Health Program, 1 original and 1 photocopy, with DOLE listed under Where to Secure. Boring and Plate Load Test Result/Report is listed if the structure is at least three storeys, 2 copies, with a licensed Civil/Geotechnical Engineer listed. For an additional storey, submit an affidavit that the foundation can carry the proposed additional floors, 2 originals.
