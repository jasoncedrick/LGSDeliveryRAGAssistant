The Building Permit checklist lists a notarized application letter; notarized Unified Building Permit Form with Civil/Architectural plans signed/sealed by a licensed Civil Engineer/Architect (5 accomplished form copies); Architectural, Civil/Structural, and Sanitary/Plumbing forms (3 form copies and 5 plan sets each). Civil/Structural plans identify a licensed Civil Engineer; Sanitary/Plumbing identifies a licensed Sanitary Engineer/Master Plumber. Architectural plans must be signed/sealed by the hired licensed professional.

Electrical: 3 accomplished form copies and 5 plan sets, signed/sealed by a Licensed Professional Electrical Engineer. Electronics: 2 form copies and 5 plan sets, signed/sealed by a Licensed Electronics Engineer. Mechanical row states "Mechanical Permit Form with 6-7 sets Mechanical Plans" signed and sealed by the hired professional "Licensed Electronics Engineer"; it separately lists "2 copies of accomplished Form" and "5 sets of plans". These are preserved as separate source statements. The user interprets the latter five sets as another submission; the row does not explicitly say additional or specify a combined total.

The checklist also lists Zoning Location Clearance and BFP Fire Safety Evaluation Certificate (1 original and 1 photocopy each); certified true copies of land title, property tax declaration and current real-property-tax receipt (1 original and 1 photocopy each); owner/applicant ID with 3 specimen signatures (1 photocopy); PRC IDs, current PTR and TIN of signing professionals (1 photocopy per professional).

Other listed technical submissions include scaled sketch/lot and vicinity plans, relocation survey plans/reports signed/sealed by a licensed Geodetic Engineer (4 copies); technical specifications signed/sealed by a licensed Architect (2 copies); bill of materials/cost estimates signed/sealed by a licensed Architect (4 copies); seismic analysis; clear detailed location photographs with landmarks or Google Maps image.

Additional clearances are conditional: DPWH for structures along a National Highway; CAAP for tall structures; DENR ECC for environmentally critical projects; drainage clearance where stated; traffic impact assessment with site development/parking plans for major development; and RROW clearance for structures along City/Barangay/Subdivision roads. These rows list 1 original and 1 photocopy and preserve their stated conditions.
