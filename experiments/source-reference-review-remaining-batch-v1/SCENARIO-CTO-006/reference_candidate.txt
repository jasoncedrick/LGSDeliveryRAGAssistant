Annex14 states an annual basic community tax of PHP5 for liable individuals plus an annual additional tax of PHP1 per PHP1,000 of income, with the additional tax capped at PHP5,000. For husband and wife the additional tax is based on their total property and total gross receipts/earnings. The cap is on additional tax, not the basic-plus-additional total.

For liable corporations, the basic annual tax is PHP500 plus additional tax capped at PHP10,000. The additional schedule is PHP2 per PHP5,000 of Philippine real-property value during the preceding year using the stated real-property-tax valuation, plus PHP2 per PHP5,000 of Philippine business gross receipts/earnings during the preceding year. Intercorporate dividends are included in gross receipts/earnings for this additional tax. The cap is on additional tax.
