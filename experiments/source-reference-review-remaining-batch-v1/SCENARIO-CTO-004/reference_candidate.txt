Annex15, Section72 lists annual fixed delivery-vehicle taxes: motorcycle/motorized tricycle PHP240; delivery jeepney/similar utility vehicle PHP600; truck, van, or any vehicle with six wheels and above PHP1,000; water vessel, barges, ferry boats, tugboats and similar watercraft PHP2,000; airplane/aircraft PHP5,000. The section applies to vehicles used by manufacturers, producers, wholesalers, dealers or retailers in delivery/distribution of the listed products and goods within the City. Preserve the six-wheel row wording when classifying a vehicle.
