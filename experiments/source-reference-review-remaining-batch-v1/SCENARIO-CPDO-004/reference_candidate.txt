Motion for Reconsideration is an appeal against denial of Locational Clearance by the Zoning Administrator. The checklist lists: Letter of Reconsideration; Denial Letter; Order of Payment; Official Receipt; Business Permit Application Form/Building Permit Application Form (for building permit); certified true copy of title(s)/Contract of Lease/Deed of Sale; site sketch with vicinity map and landmarks; Barangay Council Resolution Interposing No Objection; affidavit of non-objection of adjoining property owners; locational mapping of neighbor consent signatories; Affidavit of Undertaking; and Building Permit for a business-permit application.

Secure checklist and pre-evaluation; once documents are complete, obtain the order for the PHP600 processing fee. Submit original and photocopied official receipt with one complete set of documents. The process includes TWG site inspection/evaluation, TWG meeting with the applicant, committee deliberation, and, if approved, resolution and endorsement to OBO or Business Bureau. Claim the endorsement with attached resolution. Non-approved/deferred cases may receive NOC, NOD or disapproval.
