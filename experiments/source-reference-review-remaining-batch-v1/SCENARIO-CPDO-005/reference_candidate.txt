For online business locational clearance, the checklist lists: uploaded application form; Affidavit of Undertaking for neighbor consent with sketch map showing neighbor location (1 photocopy); Homeowners Association Certificate of No Objection; Certificate of Actual Land Occupancy for Migrant (based on RA8371); Locational Clearance for Building Permit, if applicable; PRC ID for licensed broker/License to Sell/Affidavit of Undertaking not to sell raw lots (PD957); Affidavit of Undertaking for Online Business/Booking Office/On-Job Site/On-Call/Private Garage; pictures showing working area including roads; and sketch map. Additional requirements may be required as the need arises.

The fee columns and TOTAL state None. The TOTAL is 1 Day and 50 Minutes. This includes denial-letter steps, with signature time dependent on administrator availability; it is not a guaranteed approval deadline. Approved/conditionally approved cases have their BPLS status updated and other offices then evaluate the application.
