The source describes validation for "3-storey buildings and above and for building floor area of 1,500 square meters and above only." User clarification: either category can qualify (3-storey and above OR floor area of 1,500 square meters and above). This alternative-trigger interpretation is user-supplied; the cited PDF itself uses "and" and does not independently resolve that interpretation.

Checklist: approved building plans (1 original); approved building permit (1 original); Certificate of Occupancy Form (1 original); Locational Clearance, if applicable (1 photocopy); pictures of building (1 original); Order of Payment (1 original); Official Receipt (1 original).

Filing fee is PHP150.00. The stated TOTAL is 3 Days, 2 Hours, and 10 Minutes; the final-approval step depends on availability of the Zoning Administrator.
