Portable Fire Extinguishers are regulated by the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) through its Bureau of Products Standards (BPS)
PARTS: handle; hose; pressure gauge; marking for hazard application (A, B, C); nozzle; tank.
There are 4 types of fire extinguishers available in the country. To be sure of product safety and reliability, each type must comply with the appropriate Philippine National Standards (PNS).
Dry Chemical: PNS 15-1:1989
Carbon Dioxide: PNS 15-3:1991
Foam: PNS 15-4:1991
Halon Substitute: PNS 15-5:1996

Know your product
Every fire extinguisher type has different color bands to distinguish their type. Each type is intended for use for specific flammable materials that are classified into codes:
[Representation of displayed class panels]
Dry Chemical: CLASS A FIRES; CLASS B FIRES; CLASS C FIRES.
Carbon Dioxide: CLASS B FIRES; CLASS C FIRES.
Foam: CLASS A FIRES; CLASS B FIRES.
Halon Substitute: CLASS A FIRES; CLASS B FIRES; CLASS C FIRES.
[Repeated class descriptions transcribed once; mappings above retain their source associations.]
CLASS A FIRES — Ordinary Combustibles: wood; paper; rubber; fabrics; many plastics.
CLASS B FIRES — Flamable Liquids: gasoline; oils; paint; lacquers; tar.
CLASS C FIRES — Electrical Equipment: wiring; fuse boxes; circuit breakers; appliances.

How to determine the number of fire extinguisher:
Type of Building: Low Hazard: Presence of class A combustible and class B flammables are low.
~ Offices, classrooms, churches, assembly halls, guest room areas of hotel/motels, residential, and so forth.
Minimum No. of Extinguisher: 1 piece - 10 lb Dry Chemical; or 1 piece - 10 lb Halongenated (At the option of the client)
Maximum Travel (m): 15 m
Maximum Area (m²): 200 m²

Type of Building: Moderate Hazard: Presence of class A combustible and class B flammables are moderate.
~ Restaurants, dining areas, mercantile shops, and allied storage, light manufacturing, research operations, auto showrooms, parking garages.
Minimum No. of Extinguisher: 1 piece - 10 lb Dry Chemical; or 1 piece - 10 lb Halongenated (At the option of the client)
Maximum Travel (m): 12 m
Maximum Area (m²): 100 m²

Type of Building: High Hazard: Presence of class A combustible and class B flammables are high.
~ Industrial, warehouse, gasoline stations, mercantile, cooking areas.
Minimum No. of Extinguisher: 1 piece - 10 lb Dry Chemical; or 1 piece - 10 lb Halongenated; or 1 piece - 10 lb Foam (At the option of the client)
Maximum Travel (m): 10 m
Maximum Area (m²): 75 m²

Example: An office establishment with an area of 200 square meters,dimension of 10x20 m, involving low class A combustible and low class B flammable, is required with 1 piece Dry Chemical or Halonagenated, at the option of the owner.
