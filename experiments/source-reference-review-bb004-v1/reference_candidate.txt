According to the Occupational Permit section of the thesis source charter (printed pages 104-106; PDF pages 65-67):

The charter states that an Occupational Permit is issued to any worker or employee who is not a holder of validly issued Professional Tax Receipts, whether working on a temporary or permanent basis.

Under Who may avail, it lists Filipino employees or workers aged 18 at application; foreigners may apply after securing an Alien Employment Permit from DOLE. Where the law allows a person below 18 to work, an Affidavit of Consent from the parent or guardian is required.

Renew annually on or before January 31. The charter states a 25% penalty for late renewal.

The fee table and TOTAL row state PHP 125.00 for new and updated renewal, and PHP 156.25 for late renewal. Payment is to the City Treasurer’s Office.

Application Form: Business Bureau Office, Window 1 or Public Assistance Desk. Certificate of Employment: applicant.

Checklist item 3 specifies Official Receipt (for service crew), from the City Treasurer’s Office. Separately, the process instructs walk-in applicants to submit required documents including the official receipt.

A notarized Affidavit of Consent is listed for minors below 18. For Night Clubs and related establishments and similar places of amusement, the affidavit is listed for ages 18 to 20.

Additional requirements for Entertainers, Masseurs, Bar Attendants: Birth Certificate (Philippine Statistics Authority); Valid ID of parent or qualified guardian for parent’s consent with appearance (Where to Secure: Any Notary public); Health ID (City Health).

The process note states: If the applicant is an entertainer, masseur, or bar attendant 18-20 years old, or the applicant is below 18 years old, he or she must present himself/herself for an interview. Agency action 2.2 repeats these conditions.
