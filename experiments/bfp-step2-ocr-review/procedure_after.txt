FEES TO BE PAID (one shared source cell spanning all six agency rows):
For FSEC
Application Fee: Php 200.00
FCCT = 0.1% of the verified estimated value
(materials and labor) of the building/structure or
facility but not more than Php 50,000.00
Sample computation:
FCCT = (Verified estimated value) x (.1%) = ___Php
but not more than Php 50, 000.00
Note: Section 8.3.6 of JMC 2021-01 Series of
2021—In the event that there is substantial
difference between the cost estimate of the building
or structure as declared by the owner or applicant
in the UAF from current acceptable market
construction cost per square meter or as indicated
in Table II.G.1 of PD 1096, the higher the value will
be adopted
For FSIC (Certificate of Occupancy)/FSIC (New
Business and Renewal of Business) Applica-
tion
FSIF = 15% of all fees charge by LGU/Philippine
Economic Zone Authority (PEZA) but in no case
shall be lower than Php 500.00
Sample computation:
FSIF = (All fees charged by the LGU/PEZA) x
(15%) = ______ Php but in no case shall be lower
than Php 500.00
Note: If assessment from LGUs/PEZA = 0, then
FSIF = 0
Other fees
If applicable compute the appropriate fees in
accordance with Section 12.0.0.4 of the RIRR
of RA 9514 (e.g. storage, conveyance,
hotworks, etc.) as a prerequisite for the
issuance of appropriate permits and licenses
from the local and other government agencies
concerned.
