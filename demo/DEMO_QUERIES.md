# Adviser demo queries

Practice only: these are not new benchmark labels or evidence of system superiority. Prepared 12 November 2026. Use the same intent across languages and one request at a time. Live answers are not preapproved.

## BB

- **English:** For a sole proprietorship changing its business address, does the application affidavit need to be notarized?
- **Taglish:** Kung sole proprietorship at magpapalit ng business address, kailangan bang notarized ang application affidavit?
- **Bislish:** Kung sole proprietorship ug magbalhin og business address, kinahanglan bang notarized ang application affidavit?

Presenter check: Check the CHANGE OF BUSINESS ADDRESS section, item 2; do not answer for renewal or a new permit.
Source: BB-PDF0003; Business Bureau CGD_Citizen Charter 2026_VOL.I & II.pdf; PDF page 3.

## OCBO

- **English:** When is an Excavation Permit required before a construction project starts?
- **Taglish:** Kailan kailangan ang Excavation Permit bago magsimula ang construction project?
- **Bislish:** Kanus-a kinahanglan ang Excavation Permit sa dili pa magsugod ang construction project?

Presenter check: The source says prior to commencement of excavation and construction. This is not a question about expiry.
Source: OCBO-PDF0036; OCBD Pages from CGD_Citizen Charter 2026_VOL.I & II-2.pdf; PDF page 36.

## BFP

- **English:** How much is the application fee for a Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance (FSEC)?
- **Taglish:** Magkano ang application fee para sa Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance o FSEC?
- **Bislish:** Pila ang application fee para sa Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance o FSEC?

Presenter check: Distinguish the application fee from FCCT and FSIC charges; do not present it as the total cost.
Source: BFP-PDF0001; BFP-CITIZENS-CHARTER-2022.pdf; PDF page 1.

## CPDO

- **English:** For CPDO validation of an Occupancy Permit for a 3-storey building with a 1,500-square-meter floor area, is an approved Building Permit required?
- **Taglish:** Para sa CPDO validation ng Occupancy Permit ng 3-storey building na 1,500 square meters ang floor area, kailangan ba ng approved Building Permit?
- **Bislish:** Para sa CPDO validation sa Occupancy Permit sa 3-storey building nga 1,500 square meters ang floor area, kinahanglan ba og approved Building Permit?

Presenter check: Both qualifiers are explicit. The checklist lists one original approved Building Permit; do not resolve the source AND/OR issue.
Source: CPDO-PDF0005; City Planning CGD_Citizen Charter 2026_VOL.I & II-3.pdf; PDF page 5.

## CTO

- **English:** What is the first online step to apply for an individual Community Tax Certificate or cedula?
- **Taglish:** Ano ang unang online step para kumuha ng individual Community Tax Certificate o cedula?
- **Bislish:** Unsa ang unang online step para mokuha og individual Community Tax Certificate o cedula?

Presenter check: The individual service starts at the cedula website, selecting INDIVIDUAL and entering information. Do not infer a fixed tax amount.
Source: CTO-PDF0047; City Treasurer's CGD_Citizen Charter 2026_VOL.I & II-2.pdf; PDF page 47.

## Optional ambiguity checks

- English: I need a clearance. Which one should I apply for?
- Taglish: Kailangan ko ng clearance. Alin ang dapat kong kunin?
- Bislish: Kinahanglan ko og clearance. Asa ani ang akong kuhaon?

Expected product behavior: ask which service or purpose. The current v2 demo has a documented missed-ambiguity failure; show this honestly if it occurs. Do not promise this check passes.

For a short presentation, use one English, one Taglish and one Bislish question. Keep the remaining questions for discussion; selection is for demonstration time, not comparative performance reporting.
